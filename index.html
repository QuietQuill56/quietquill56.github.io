Ami Personal Dashboard

Ami Personal Dashboard is a private dashboard for its owner's daily schedule and personal planning. It runs locally on the owner's Mac.

Its Google Calendar connection displays events from the calendars selected by the owner, including scheduled work blocks and birthday reminders. Calendar access is read-only: the integration does not create, edit or delete Google Calendar events.

This public page explains the application. It does not provide access to the dashboard, calendar events or personal records, and it does not offer public registration or sign-in.
